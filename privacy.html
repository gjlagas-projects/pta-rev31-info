Privacy policy

pta-rev31, a personal photo archive tool. Last updated 24 October 2026.

Who uses it

Only its owner. pta-rev31 is a personal tool, not offered to the public. It signs in to the owner's own Google account and no one else's.

What it accesses

  • Google Drive: the photos, videos and folders of the owner's archive, in the owner's own Drive. It reads files dropped into the archive, copies and moves them into dated folders, and writes notes (tags, dates, file details) next to them.
  • Gmail (send only): it sends email to the owner: a daily summary and cost alerts. It cannot read, search or delete any mail.

Where the data goes

  • Files stay in the owner's Google Drive. Work in progress is kept briefly in the owner's own Google Cloud project and deleted within days.
  • A catalog of the files (names, dates, tags) is kept in a database in the owner's own Google Cloud project.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with or sent to anyone else. There are no ads and no analytics.

Google user data

pta-rev31's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping access

The owner can remove pta-rev31's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

The support email shown on pta-rev31's Google sign-in screen.
