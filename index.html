pta-rev31

A personal photo archive tool.

pta-rev31 organizes one family's photos and videos. It runs for its owner only, on the owner's own Google Cloud project, and works on files in the owner's own Google Drive.

It signs in to one Google account, the owner's, with two permissions:

  • Google Drive: to read new photos dropped into the archive's folders, file them by date, and keep notes (tags, dates) beside them.
  • Gmail, send only: to send the owner a daily summary and cost alerts. It cannot read mail.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
